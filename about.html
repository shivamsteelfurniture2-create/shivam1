About Us

Furniture Built Around How You Actually Live.

Shivam Furniture is a Wagholi-based interior design and modular furniture studio. We design, craft and install modular kitchens, wardrobes and complete home interiors for families across Pune — with honest pricing and no shortcuts on quality.

Our Story

Shivam Furniture was founded in 2010 by Papu Ram Bishnoi with a simple idea: interiors don't need to be complicated or overpriced to be beautiful. Working out of Wagholi on Nagar Road, we've grown project by project — kitchen by kitchen, wardrobe by wardrobe — earning trust through consistent quality and transparent pricing rather than heavy advertising.

Every project starts with a real conversation about how a family cooks, stores, relaxes and lives in their home, and the design follows from that — not the other way around. Over 1000+ projects later, that founding idea still guides how we work.
